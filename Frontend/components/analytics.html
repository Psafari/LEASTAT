Terms & Definitions

What is ADR (Average Daily Rate)?
ADR stands for Average Daily Rate. It represents the average rental income per occupied room in a given time period. It's calculated by dividing total room revenue by the number of rooms sold.
What is RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)?
RevPAR is a key performance metric that measures revenue generated per available room. It's calculated by multiplying ADR by occupancy rate, or by dividing total room revenue by total available rooms.
What is Occupancy Rate?
Occupancy rate is the percentage of available rooms that are occupied during a specific time period. It's calculated by dividing the number of occupied rooms by the total number of available rooms.
What is Lead Time?
Lead time refers to the number of days between when a booking is made and the actual check-in date. It helps understand booking patterns and customer behavior.
What is Length of Stay (LOS)?
Length of Stay (LOS) is the average number of nights guests stay during their visit. It's calculated by dividing total room nights by the number of reservations.
What are Revenue Targets?
Revenue targets are predetermined financial goals set for different business segments (accommodations, health services, restaurants) to measure performance against expected outcomes.
